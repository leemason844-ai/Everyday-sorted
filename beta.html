Beta testing notice

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Early-access beta

Welcome to the Everyday Sorted early-access beta. The app is free to try while we test reliability and improve the experience.

What to expect

You can create a seven-day meal plan, swap recipes, adjust portions, make shopping lists and mark ingredients already at home. Some recommendations, quantities, prices and ingredient matching may be inaccurate. Check everything before buying ingredients or cooking.

Keep a backup

Your plans live on your device, not in an online account. Use More → Back up my data before clearing browser data, changing devices or reinstalling. The Safari website and an installed Home Screen app may have separate data.

Help us improve

Use More → Help improve Everyday Sorted to send suggestions or report bugs. Email is optional. Avoid sharing private or sensitive details. We cannot promise individual replies or a particular fix date.

Not allergy-safe

Dietary filters and cupboard matching are not suitable for managing allergies. Always check product labels and ingredients independently.

What happens next?

Features and wording may change as we test the beta. Any future paid features will be clearly explained before purchase; no subscription is required for this beta.
